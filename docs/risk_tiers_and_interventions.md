# Risk Tiers and Intervention Recommendations (Step 2.9)

## 1. Purpose
This document outlines the operational implementation of risk tiers and intervention recommendations based on the existing predictive model. The purpose is to convert the raw predictive risk probability into categorical risk tiers to provide supportive recommendations for human advisors and academic teams.

## 2. Input Risk Probability
The input is the `risk_probability` generated by the uncalibrated XGBoost model (the selected base probability model). The probability ranges between 0.0 and 1.0. 

## 3. Four Risk Tiers
The risk probability is categorized into four distinct operational risk tiers:
- LOW
- MODERATE
- HIGH
- CRITICAL

## 4. Exact Threshold Boundaries
The thresholds for each tier are pre-specified and configurable via `configs/intervention_config.yaml`. The current operational boundaries are:
- **LOW**: 0.0 <= `risk_probability` < 0.20
- **MODERATE**: 0.20 <= `risk_probability` < 0.30
- **HIGH**: 0.30 <= `risk_probability` < 0.50
- **CRITICAL**: 0.50 <= `risk_probability` <= 1.0

## 5. Intervention Policy
Intervention recommendations are generated for each tier:
- **LOW**:
  - Routine monitoring
  - Provide standard learning resources
  - No immediate escalation
- **MODERATE**:
  - Academic check-in
  - Study-planning support
  - Recommend relevant learning resources
  - Monitor engagement
- **HIGH**:
  - Advisor/tutor outreach
  - Personalized academic support
  - Review recent engagement/assessment activity
  - Consider follow-up by a human advisor
- **CRITICAL**:
  - Priority human review
  - Advisor intervention
  - Individualized academic support
  - Review relevant academic and engagement information
  - Consider immediate supportive follow-up

## 6. Human-Review Policy
- **LOW**: False
- **MODERATE**: False
- **HIGH**: True
- **CRITICAL**: True

This is an operational policy rule to route students for human attention, not a mathematical claim that the model is certain. 

## 7. Priority Score
The system assigns a `priority_score` which is currently identical to the `risk_probability`.

## 9. Limitations
- Risk tiers are operational categories, not clinical or definitive diagnoses.
- The risk probability does not prove that a student will definitively withdraw; it merely reflects historical correlations.
- Intervention recommendations are supportive suggestions designed to assist human advisors in structuring their outreach, not automated punitive actions.
- Thresholds were pre-specified operationally rather than optimized via outcome dredging on the validation set.
- The system should not automatically take punitive actions against students.
- The final holdout (`final_holdout.parquet`) and historical locked test sets remain strictly untouched during this process.
